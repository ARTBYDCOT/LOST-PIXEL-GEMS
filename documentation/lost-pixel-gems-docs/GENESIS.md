## 0. Origin — Experiment R3
Everything begins with **[Experiment R3](docs/ORIGIN.md)**, the origin of the artistic concept: the pixel as *matter*, accumulated and mobilized into art. The visual record of the process lives in the site's **Creative Process** page (`web/process.html` · [live](https://lostpixelgems.space/process.html)).

## 1. Manifesto & vision
Free to create — **no wallet, no subscription** — only for the love of voxel & pixel art. Permanence is earned by burning PIXELS. The endgame is a **fully on-chain museum**: a permanent collection that outlives every server. → **[MANIFESTO](docs/MANIFESTO.md)**

## 2. The world — Lost Pixel City
A honeycomb of gem-portals on an open meadow: unique per-gem swirling spirals, NFT frames at eye height, a wireframe neon skyline, an animated galaxy sky.

![Lost Pixel City](docs/img/01-city.png)

## 3. The tool — the free Voxel Editor
A full, browser-native MagicaVoxel-style editor: brushes, shapes, selection/wand, layers, 3D gizmo, grow/shrink, AI shape prompts, multi-format import/export, **animation & play mode** — all free. → **[TOOLS](docs/TOOLS.md)**

![Voxel Editor](docs/img/02-editor.png)

## 4. The room — walk it in first person
Step *through a portal* into a collector's editable voxel room, as a free-flying spectator — the same engine as the editor, mirroring live edits.

![First-person room](docs/img/04-room.png)

## 5. Be a pixel — the public map
Enter `lpg-map` as a **single pixel** and roam the shared 8000×256 world where kept constructions live forever.

## 6. The economy — deflationary by design
Burn PIXELS → mint voxel worlds → grow a **~8,000,000-voxel micro-universe.** Supply only ever decreases; value accrues to permanence and to the commons. → **[ECONOMY](docs/ECONOMY.md)** (diagrams + tables)

## 7. The chain — open & auditable
Six live collections + protocol contracts (PIXELS, VoxelLAND, MapArchive, view-only Exhibition) **open for development and audit.** → **[CONTRACTS](docs/CONTRACTS.md)** · **[CONTRIBUTING](CONTRIBUTING.md)** · **[SECURITY](SECURITY.md)**
